# Documentation D5: historical records and final review

## Intent

Keep research and evidence useful without presenting old conditions as today's operating procedure. Close the editorial review with explicit coverage and remaining implementation findings.

## Constraints

- Preserve historical measurements, attribution, human amendments and failure observations.
- Do not replay experiments or relabel past results as current verification.
- Use a dated-scope notice where needed, not a blanket warning on every paragraph.
- Distinguish content review from structural inventory checks and rendered-page inspection.

## Work

1. Review research/evidence entry points, openings and links for ambiguity about current operation.
2. Provide navigation to current guides and clearly scoped historical studies/results.
3. Repair cross-references and stale status claims at the owning indexes. Preserve original experiment conditions.
4. Check the combined developer/operator/model/observability/design path against authorship guidance.
5. Render representative changed pages, check local targets/anchors, and record exactly what was inspected.
6. Update the review/inventory and roadmap; retain separate implementation and live-validation gaps.

## Acceptance criteria

- Historical records identify their purpose and conditions and route readers to current procedures.
- No original measurement or recorded failure is erased or upgraded into a current claim.
- The review names inspected pages, checks, remaining findings and untested execution paths.
- Changed batches are committed and available as separate PRs; current status has one owner.
